The original font's tnum (tabular figure) alternates are selected in the Unicode
cmap for 0–9. The result is subset to timer characters and renamed Cube Lab Timer.
This makes genuine fixed-width numeral designs available to egui, whose text
renderer does not apply OpenType tnum substitution. No glyph outlines or numeric
metrics were stretched or synthesized. All ten digits retain 600-unit advances.
